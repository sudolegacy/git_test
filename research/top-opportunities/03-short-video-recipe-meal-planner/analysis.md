# 03 — Short-Video Recipe Import + Weekly Meal Planner (Score 71/100)

**Concept:** recipe manager and weekly meal planner whose hook is importing recipes from TikTok, Reels and YouTube videos via the share sheet, with AI normalisation (ingredients, quantities, steps), scaling and substitutions, a weekly plan, an aisle-sorted grocery list and household sharing. Offered with annual and one-time "Lifetime" options to capture displaced Mealime and Paprika users.

**Pricing:** $7.99/month, $39.99/year, $79 lifetime.

Evidence labels: **V** verified (primary/press), **3P** third-party estimate, **U** unverified, **Calc** own calculation. Research date 12 Nov 2026.

## 2. Market and timing

- **Live displacement window:** Mealime's own site states "Mealime will shut down on October 21, 2026" (V); owner Albertsons announced it 2 Sep 2026, Pro was free until shutdown, recipes moved to "Meals Hub" inside Albertsons grocery apps, personal data deleted (3P). Paprika 4 (6 Nov 2026): Premium $14.99/yr for new users with 7-day trial, $9.99/yr for existing owners; Premium adds TikTok/IG/FB/YouTube import, scanning and family accounts; free tier limited to 50 recipes on one device; Paprika 3 was $4.99 iOS / $29.99 Mac one-time (V). Long-time users were critical and a patch softened it (V, German press).
- **Demand signals:** #easyrecipe 3.2–4.1B tracked views and #mealprep ~1B (Toklytics partial counts, 3P/U). ReciMe claims "15m+ cooks" (self-claim). Paprika, Osta, Flavorish, Honeydew and MyRecipes all sell social import.
- **New entrants proving the funnel (all 3P/U, marketing-sourced):** TapCook launched 17 Jul 2026 → $50K MRR with 100K downloads; a LinkedIn post claims ~$110K/mo with no ads. Potto launched 12 Jun 2026 → claims $100K MRR from one 25.8M-view TikTok (Scrolled estimates $37.4K, range $8.7–173K). NeatEat, mise and Herbi similar; "3 apps pulled $220K MRR in 60 days" (Social Growth Engineers). Prices: TapCook $9.99/mo or $29.99/yr; NeatEat $12.99/mo or $7.99/wk; Potto ~$9.99/wk.

## 3. Customer problem

1. **Who:** home cooks who find recipes on TikTok/Reels/YouTube, households planning weekly meals, displaced Mealime/Paprika users.
2. **Frequency:** several times a week (saving) and weekly (planning/shopping).
3. **Importance:** moderate — convenience and food-budget control; strong emotional pull from short-form content.
4. **What they use now:** ReciMe, Paprika, AnyList, Plan to Eat, Samsung Food, Crouton, Mela, screenshots and saved-video folders.
5. **Cost of current solutions:** free to $59.99/yr; weekly plans $7.99–9.99/wk on the new viral apps.
6. **Why insufficient:** import failures ("it will often say there wasn't a recipe in the caption so it used the AI when there is definitely a full, detailed recipe in the caption" — ReciMe review 11/05/2025, V; "some recipes it still gives me an error, even though the ingredients are listed" — 01/13/2025, V); price ("a bit pricey" — 11/17/2024, V); among 143 one- and two-star reviews of five budget planners: 31% price, 28% billing/trial problems, 23% paywall before value, 20% AI-generated recipes/photos (3P). Paprika users cite no free tier and no saving from videos (3P). Reviewers prefer one-time Paprika/Mela over subscription AnyList/Crouton (U).
7. **Will they pay:** yes, at $30–60/yr — but price-sensitive and hostile to weekly billing.

## 5. Distribution and viral potential

- **Short-form video is the content and the channel.** Creator posts a recipe → viewer imports it in-app → the app is demonstrated in the act of using it. Food creators can be paid to add "save this in [App]" CTAs; the app can surface a creator's recipes as a collection (with permission).
- **Proof:** Potto's one-video spike (U); TapCook's no-ads growth claim (U); ReciMe's #1 grossing position (3P).
- **Displacement capture:** "Switching from Mealime/Paprika?" landing pages and in-app importers (Paprika export, Mealime CSV where users saved it).
- **ASO:** "recipe keeper", "meal planner", "tiktok recipe", "save recipes from instagram"; Food & Drink keywords less contested than "invoice".
- **Referral:** household sharing is a natural invite loop (partner/roommate joins the plan).
- **Paid:** Productivity-proxy CPI $3.58 → ≈$179/payer at 2% conversion, ≈$33 at 10.7% — against $12.74 net on a $14.99 annual anchor or ~$34 net on $39.99 (red-team Calc). **Paid acquisition is untenable; organic-only.**

---

## 6. Monetisation design

- **Model:** freemium + annual subscription + Lifetime option (the clearest one-time-vs-subscription tension in the research: Paprika backlash, Fantastical backlash, Stitch Fiddle's undo paywall).
- **Free:** unlimited manual recipes, limited video imports per week (match ReciMe's 5), basic list.
- **Paid ($7.99/mo, $39.99/yr, $79 lifetime):** unlimited imports, AI scaling/substitutions, weekly planner, aisle-sorted list, household sharing, nutrition estimates, multi-device sync.
- **No weekly billing** — it is the top complaint theme and the Apple-enforcement pattern (Cal AI pulled Apr 2026, V).
- **Trial:** 7-day; longer trials convert better (RevenueCat V).
- **Renewals/retention:** Sunday planning reminder; "cooked this week" streaks; seasonal collections; export always available.
- **Why subscription is justified:** weekly planning and ongoing imports recur; occasional importing does not — hence the Lifetime option for collectors.

## 8. Product blueprint

**Positioning:** "Every recipe you've ever saved, finally cookable. Import from TikTok, plan your week, shop in one list — one price, no weekly billing."

**USP:** reliable share-sheet import with transparent fallbacks (caption → transcript → on-device OCR of the user's own screen capture), AI scaling/substitutions, weekly planning, household sharing, and a Lifetime option.

**Core functionality**
- Share-sheet import from TikTok/Reels/YouTube/web; paste caption; screenshot/screen-recording import via on-device OCR and speech-to-text
- AI normalisation to structured recipe (ingredients with quantities/units, steps, time, servings), with confidence flags and easy correction
- Scaling, unit conversion, substitutions (dietary), nutrition estimate (labelled approximate)
- Weekly planner (drag recipes to days), leftovers logic
- Grocery list sorted by aisle; pantry check; household sharing
- Collections, tags, search; creator collections (permissioned)
- Cook mode (screen stays on, step-by-step, timers)
- Import from Paprika/Mealime/CSV; export always available

**Premium:** unlimited imports, planner, AI features, sharing, sync.

**Onboarding:** "share a video you saved this week" → recipe appears → save → "plan it for Tuesday" → list generated — under 2 minutes; consent screen naming the AI provider (5.1.2(i)).

**Main screens:** Saved · Plan (week) · List · Cook · Discover (creators) · Settings.

**Expansion:** creator storefronts/collections, grocery-delivery integrations, nutrition goals (GLP-1 protein mode links to opportunity 02), smart-display/tablet mode.

## 9. Technical architecture

- Expo / React Native (New Architecture) + TypeScript; iOS Share Extension and Android share intents; Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage, Realtime); RevenueCat (incl. non-consumable Lifetime); PostHog + Sentry.
- **Import pipeline (lower-risk design):** user-initiated share-sheet URL → fetch *only* publicly exposed caption/oEmbed metadata where permitted → user-pasted caption → on-device OCR (Vision/ML Kit) and on-device speech-to-text (Apple SpeechAnalyzer / Android SpeechRecognizer / whisper.cpp) of the user's own screen capture → Claude Haiku 5.5 structuring with JSON schema and confidence scores. Avoid server-side downloading/scraping of video.
- **Legal constraints (V):** TikTok Terms (updated 1 Dec 2025) prohibit "automated scripts to collect information" and downloading/copying content without consent; TikTok oEmbed returns embed HTML, and Embed Videos "will be deprecated soon"; YouTube Developer Policies prohibit downloading/storing audiovisual content without approval and cap API data caching at 30 days; Meta/Instagram terms not read in primary form (3P summary bars automated collection). Competitors scrape anyway; a user-initiated, on-device approach is lower risk but **needs counsel review — not legal advice**.
- AI cost: cents per import; summaries via Haiku; vision via Sonnet 5.5 for screenshots when OCR is insufficient.
- **Difficulty:** medium. **Time to MVP:** 3 weeks. **Backend complexity:** low–medium. **Platform limits:** share-extension memory limits on iOS; on-device STT accuracy varies by platform; platform ToS/API churn. **Maintenance:** import heuristics break when platforms change — budget continuous upkeep.

## 10. Risks and red-team

1. **Price anchor collapse.** Paprika offers comparable imports at $14.99/yr; a premium-priced clone needs more than nicer AI — the planner + household + Lifetime bundle must carry the price.
2. **Dependence on platforms you don't control** for both import (ToS, API deprecation) and distribution (TikTok algorithm). A share sheet is not blanket contractual immunity.
3. **Saving ≠ cooking.** Collection growth can masquerade as engagement while weekly planning and renewal stay poor; instrument "cooked" events, not saves.
4. **Price sensitivity is high** (31% of complaints); removing deceptive billing improves satisfaction, not willingness to pay a premium.
5. **AI distrust:** invented recipes and wrong quantities (20% of complaints) actively reduce trust — show confidence, make correction one tap, never fabricate when the caption has the recipe.
6. **CAC:** at $14.99/yr net $12.74, even the $33 hard-paywall illustrative CAC exceeds two years of revenue; at $39.99 it consumes the first year. Organic-only.
7. **Mobile web would often suffice**; native earns its keep with share-sheet integration, offline recipes and kitchen-friendly cook mode.
8. **Clone flood:** four sub-five-month-old planners in the top 10 means dozens more behind them; the window is measured in months.

**Red-team (GPT-6.1) verdict:** weekly planning can justify a subscription; occasional importing cannot. Fund only with a clear organic creator loop and instrumented cooking (not saving) retention.

## 11. Execution outline (if pursued)

- **Weeks 1–3:** MVP (share-sheet import with on-device pipeline, structured editor, planner, list, household); counsel review of import approach; Paprika/Mealime importers.
- **Week 4:** launch to displaced-user landing pages ("switching from Mealime/Paprika"); 10 food micro-creators demo importing their own recipes; no paid ads.
- **Months 2–3:** gate — ≥4% of installs paying within 35 days, ≥50% of payers logging a "cooked" event weekly, month-3 payer retention ≥55%; otherwise stop or fold into opportunity 02's nutrition mode.
- **Months 4–6:** creator collections, grocery integration test, Lifetime-tier cohort analysis vs annual.
